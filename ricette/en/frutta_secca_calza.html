Stocking nuts and figs

In the past, the Befana's stocking held mostly nuts, dried fruit, mandarins and a few sweets: the simple gifts of country families.

Servings6
Prep20 min
Cook10 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Tree nuts

Ingredients

  • Dried figs · 12
  • Walnuts · 6
  • Almonds · 12
  • Honey · 2 tbsp
  • Orange zest · zest of 1
  • Cinnamon · 1 pinch
  • Mandarins · 4

Step by step

  1. Split the dried figs in half without separating them completely.
  2. Put half a walnut and an almond in each fig with a pinch of cinnamon and a little orange zest, then close it.
  3. Bake the figs at 180 °C for 10 minutes and brush them with honey while still warm.
  4. Once cool, wrap them in paper bags and put them in the stocking with the mandarins and a few whole walnuts and almonds.
Chef's tip. You can also use dates or prunes: stuffed with a walnut they're just as good.
