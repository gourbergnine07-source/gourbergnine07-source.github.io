Gnocchi di susine

It is a dish of the Austro-Hungarian tradition, where sweet 'Knödel' are popular from Vienna to Prague.

Servings4
Prep50 min
Cook45 min
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Floury potatoes · 1 kg
  • Type 00 flour · 250 g
  • Egg · 1
  • Small plums · 12–16
  • Sugar cubes · 12–16
  • Butter · 100 g
  • Breadcrumbs · 80 g
  • Cinnamon and sugar · to taste
  • Salt · 1 pinch

Step by step

  1. Boil the potatoes, mash them while hot and let cool.
  2. Knead the potatoes with flour, egg and salt into a soft dough.
  3. Pit the plums without splitting them and put inside each one a sugar cube with a pinch of cinnamon.
  4. Wrap each plum in a disc of dough, sealing well.
  5. Cook the dumplings in salted boiling water for about 10 minutes after they float.
  6. Toast the breadcrumbs in butter with sugar and cinnamon and roll the dumplings in the coating.
Chef's tip. In Trieste they are eaten as a sweet first course, in summer, when the plums are ripe.
