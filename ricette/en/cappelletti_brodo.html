Cappelletti in broth

In Umbria, as in much of central Italy, Christmas lunch often starts with cappelletti in capon broth, the rooster raised especially for the holidays.

Servings6
Prep1 h 30 min
Cook3 h 4 min
DifficultyHard

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk, Celery

Ingredients

  • Capon (for the broth) · 1
  • Onion · 1
  • Carrots · 2
  • Celery · 1 stalk
  • Flour · 300 g
  • Eggs (pasta) · 3
  • Pork loin · 150 g
  • Grated Parmigiano · 80 g
  • Egg (filling) · 1
  • Nutmeg · 1 pinch

Step by step

  1. Broth: put the capon in cold water with the onion, carrots and celery, simmer for about 3 hours, then strain.
  2. Filling: brown the pork in pieces, then mince it finely with a little of the boiled capon meat; add Parmigiano, egg and nutmeg.
  3. Knead the flour and eggs, roll out a thin sheet and cut 4 cm squares. Put a little filling in the centre, fold into a triangle and join the corners into a little hat.
  4. Cook the cappelletti in the boiling capon broth for 3–4 minutes and serve with Parmigiano.
Chef's tip. Don't throw away the boiled capon: with a salad or a little mostarda it becomes the main course of the lunch.
