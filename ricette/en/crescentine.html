Crescentine (tigelle)

The name 'tigelle' originally referred to the terracotta discs used to cook them; the bread is called 'crescentina'.

Servings4
Prep20 min
Cook30 min
Rest2 h 30 min
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Gluten, Milk

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Type 0 flour · 500 g
  • Lukewarm milk · 150 ml
  • Lukewarm water · 150 ml
  • Lard (or oil) · 50 g
  • Fresh brewer's yeast · 12 g
  • Salt · 10 g
  • Sugar · 1 pinch
  • Mortadella, cold cuts or soft cheese · for filling

Step by step

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the milk and water with the sugar.
  2. Knead with flour, lard and salt until you get a soft, smooth dough. Let rise 1.5–2 hours.
  3. Roll out the dough about 5 mm thick and cut discs of 8–9 cm. Let them rest 30 minutes.
  4. Cook them in the special tigelliera pan or a covered non-stick pan over medium-low heat, 4–5 minutes per side. In batches: about 30 minutes in all.
  5. Open them in half while still warm and fill with mortadella, cold cuts or the 'cunza' (pounded lard with garlic and rosemary).
Chef's tip. Serve them hot, freshly cooked: it is a dish to share at the table.
