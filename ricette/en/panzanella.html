Panzanella

Servings4
Prep20 min
Rest50 min
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Stale Tuscan bread · 400 g
  • Ripe tomatoes · 4
  • Red onion · 1
  • Cucumber · 1
  • Basil · 1 small bunch
  • Red wine vinegar · 2 tablespoons
  • Extra-virgin olive oil · 6 tablespoons
  • Salt and pepper · to taste

Step by step

  1. Soak the bread in cold water for 10 minutes, then squeeze it well with your hands and crumble it into a bowl.
  2. Slice the onion thinly and leave it 10 minutes in water with a splash of vinegar, to make it milder.
  3. Cut the tomatoes and cucumber into pieces.
  4. Combine everything with the bread and the torn basil. Dress with oil, vinegar, salt and pepper.
  5. Let rest in the fridge 30 minutes before serving.
Chef's tip. It is the fresh dish of the Tuscan summer: use very ripe tomatoes.
Did you know? Already in the Renaissance, the painter Bronzino praised in verse bread dressed with onion and oil — the ancestor of panzanella.

Allergens: Gluten, Sulphites
