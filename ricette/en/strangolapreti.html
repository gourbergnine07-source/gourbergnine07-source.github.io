Strangolapreti

The name comes from the gluttonous priests who, during the Council of Trent, ate them until they 'strangled'.

Servings4
Prep40 min
Cook9 min
Rest1 h
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Ingredients

  • Stale bread · 300 g
  • Milk · 200 ml
  • Spinach (or chard) · 400 g
  • Eggs · 2
  • Flour · 50 g
  • Grated cheese (Trentingrana) · 60 g + to serve
  • Butter · 80 g
  • Sage · 8 leaves
  • Nutmeg and salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Soak the bread in the milk for 1 hour.
  2. Boil the spinach, squeeze it very well and chop it.
  3. Knead bread, spinach, eggs, flour, cheese, nutmeg and salt.
  4. Shape elongated gnocchi of 4–5 cm.
  5. Cook them in salted boiling water for 3–4 minutes, until they float.
  6. Dress them with butter melted with sage and grated cheese.
Chef's tip. Squeeze the spinach well: if the dough is too wet, the gnocchi fall apart.
