Schiacciata all'uva

It is the sweet of the Tuscan grape harvest: the grapes are crushed into the dough, hence the name.

Servings6
Prep30 min
Cook55 min
Rest2 h 30 min
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Gluten

Ingredients

  • Type 0 flour · 500 g
  • Lukewarm water · 300 ml
  • Fresh brewer's yeast · 10 g (or 3 g dried)
  • Black grapes (canaiola or fragola) · 800 g
  • Sugar · 100 g
  • Extra-virgin olive oil · 6 tablespoons
  • Rosemary (optional) · 1 sprig
  • Salt · 1 pinch

Step by step

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the water, add flour, 2 tablespoons of oil and salt. Knead for 10 minutes until smooth.
  2. Cover and let rise 2 hours in a warm place, until doubled.
  3. Divide the dough in two. Roll out the first half into a greased pan (about 30×40 cm).
  4. Cover with half the grapes and half the sugar, then a drizzle of oil.
  5. Roll out the second half of dough on top, seal the edges and cover with the remaining grapes, pressing them lightly, the sugar, the oil and the rosemary.
  6. Let rest 30 minutes.
  7. Bake in a static oven at 190 °C for 50–60 minutes, until golden and caramelized.
Chef's tip. It is made during the grape harvest, in September and October. Eat it warm.
