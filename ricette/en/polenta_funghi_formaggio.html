Journey city: Trento

Polenta con funghi e formaggio

Servings4
Prep15 min
Cook1 h
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Cornmeal for polenta · 300 g
  • Water · 1.2 liters
  • Mixed mushrooms (porcini, chanterelles) · 500 g
  • Garlic · 1 clove
  • Butter · 30 g
  • Extra-virgin olive oil · 2 tablespoons
  • Chopped parsley · 1 tablespoon
  • Alpine dairy cheese (or Puzzone di Moena) · 200 g
  • Salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Bring the salted water to a boil and pour in the flour in a rain, whisking.
  2. Cook the polenta 40–50 minutes over low heat, stirring often with a wooden spoon.
  3. Clean the mushrooms with a cloth, slice them and sauté in oil, butter and garlic for 10 minutes. Add salt and parsley.
  4. Serve the hot polenta with the mushrooms on top and slices of cheese, which will melt with the heat.
Chef's tip. Only use mushrooms bought or checked by an expert mycologist.
Did you know? In the mountain dairies of Trentino, polenta was cooked in a copper cauldron hung over the fire.

Allergens: Milk
