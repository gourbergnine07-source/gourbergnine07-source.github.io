Fettunta

'Fettunta' means 'oily slice': it is the Tuscan way to taste the new oil in the olive mills.

Servings4
Prep5 min
Cook5 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Gluten

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Tuscan bread · 4 slices, 2 cm thick
  • Garlic · 1–2 cloves
  • New extra-virgin olive oil · 8 tablespoons
  • Coarse salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Toast the bread over embers, on the grill or in a cast-iron pan, until crisp outside.
  2. Rub the garlic on the warm surface of the bread.
  3. Add a pinch of salt and plenty of extra-virgin oil.
Chef's tip. The best moment is November, with the freshly pressed oil.
