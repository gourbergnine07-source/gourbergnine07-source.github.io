Fagioli all'uccelletto

They are called this because garlic and sage are the same aromas once used to cook game — the 'little birds'.

Servings4
Prep10 min
Cook27 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens:

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Boiled cannellini beans · 600 g
  • Peeled tomatoes or purée · 300 g
  • Sage · 6–8 leaves
  • Garlic · 2 cloves
  • Extra-virgin olive oil · 5 tablespoons
  • Salt and pepper · to taste

Step by step

  1. In a pan, heat the oil with the crushed garlic and sage for 2 minutes.
  2. Add the tomato and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Add the drained beans, salt and pepper.
  4. Cook over low heat for 15–20 minutes, until the sauce thickens.
Chef's tip. Excellent as a side for sausage or a Florentine steak.
