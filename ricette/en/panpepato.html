Panpepato

In Umbria, panpepato is made for Christmas, especially in Terni: honey, nuts, chocolate and a pinch of pepper, which gives it its name.

Servings12
Prep40 min
Cook25 min
Rest10 min
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Tree nuts, Gluten

Ingredients

  • Walnuts · 150 g
  • Almonds · 150 g
  • Hazelnuts · 100 g
  • Raisins · 100 g
  • Candied orange and citron peel · 80 g
  • Honey · 200 g
  • Dark chocolate · 100 g
  • Unsweetened cocoa · 30 g
  • Flour · 100 g
  • Cinnamon · 1 tsp
  • Nutmeg · 1 pinch
  • Black pepper · 1 pinch

Step by step

  1. Toast and roughly chop the walnuts, almonds and hazelnuts; soak the raisins in warm water for 10 minutes.
  2. Melt the honey with the chocolate over low heat.
  3. In a bowl, combine the nuts, squeezed raisins, candied peel, cocoa, flour and spices, then pour in the warm honey and mix well.
  4. With wet hands, shape small round loaves, place them on baking paper and bake at 180 °C for about 25 minutes.
Chef's tip. Panpepato gets better after a few days: keep it wrapped in paper in a cool place.
