Valeggio tortellini

Servings6
Prep1 h 30 min
Cook18 min
Rest30 min
DifficultyHard

Ingredients

  • Flour · 300 g
  • Eggs (pasta) · 3
  • Minced meat (beef, pork, chicken) · 300 g
  • Red wine · 3 tbsp
  • Grated Parmigiano · 50 g
  • Egg (filling) · 1
  • Nutmeg · 1 pinch
  • Butter · 80 g
  • Sage · 8 leaves

Step by step

  1. Filling: brown the minced meat in a little butter, add the red wine and cook for 15 minutes; once cool, mix in the Parmigiano, egg and nutmeg.
  2. Knead the flour and eggs and let rest for 30 minutes; then roll out a very thin, almost see-through sheet.
  3. Cut squares of about 3 cm, add a tiny bit of filling, fold into a triangle and knot the corners around your finger: they should be small and thin.
  4. Cook the tortellini in salted water for 2–3 minutes and dress them with melted butter and sage, or serve them in broth.
Chef's tip. Lightly flour the tray where you put the tortellini so they don't stick while you make the rest.
Did you know? In Valeggio sul Mincio, near Verona, these paper-thin tortellini are also called «love knots»: every June, a very long table is set on the Visconti bridge so everyone can eat them together.

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk
