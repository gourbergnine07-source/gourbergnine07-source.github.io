Journey city: Venezia

Frittelle veneziane

Servings6
Prep30 min
Cook30 min
Rest2 h
DifficultyMedium

Ingredients

  • Type 00 flour · 500 g
  • Lukewarm milk · 250 ml
  • Eggs · 2
  • Sugar · 60 g
  • Fresh brewer's yeast · 15 g
  • Raisins · 100 g
  • Pine nuts · 40 g
  • Lemon zest · 1
  • Rum or grappa · 2 tablespoons
  • Salt · 1 pinch
  • Seed oil for frying · to taste
  • Sugar for coating · to taste

Step by step

  1. Soak the raisins in the rum.
  2. Dissolve the yeast in the lukewarm milk. Add flour, eggs, sugar and salt and mix into a soft, sticky dough.
  3. Add the raisins, pine nuts and lemon zest.
  4. Cover and let rise 2 hours, until doubled.
  5. Heat the oil to 170 °C. Drop the dough by spoonfuls and fry 3–4 minutes, turning the fritters until golden. In batches: about 30 minutes in all.
  6. Drain on paper and roll them in sugar.
Chef's tip. They are the sweet of the Venezia Carnival: eat them warm.
Did you know? In Venezia, 'fritole' were so loved that a guild of 'fritoleri', street fryers, existed.

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk, Tree nuts
