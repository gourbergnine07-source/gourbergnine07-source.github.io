Journey city: Trento

Smacafam

The name means 'hunger killer': a filling dish made at Carnival.

Servings6
Prep20 min
Cook45 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Gluten, Milk

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Buckwheat flour · 200 g
  • Type 0 flour · 100 g
  • Milk · 750 ml
  • Luganega (Trentino sausage) · 300 g
  • Lardo or pancetta · 50 g
  • Onion · 1
  • Butter · 30 g
  • Salt and pepper · to taste

Step by step

  1. Mix the flours with the milk and salt into a smooth, lump-free batter.
  2. Sauté the chopped onion and lard in the butter, then add them to the batter with half the sausage in pieces.
  3. Pour into a greased tin and spread the rest of the sausage on top.
  4. Bake at 180 °C for 40–45 minutes, until a golden crust forms.
Chef's tip. It is eaten warm, cut into squares, even the day after.
