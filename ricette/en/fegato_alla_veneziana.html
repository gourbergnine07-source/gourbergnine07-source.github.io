Journey city: Venezia

Fegato alla veneziana

Servings4
Prep15 min
Cook33 min
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Veal liver in thin slices · 600 g
  • White onions · 600 g
  • Butter · 30 g
  • Extra-virgin olive oil · 3 tablespoons
  • Chopped parsley · 1 tablespoon
  • Salt and pepper · to taste

Step by step

  1. Slice the onions very thin and stew them with oil and butter over low heat for 30 minutes, adding a splash of water if they stick.
  2. Cut the liver into strips.
  3. Raise the heat, add the liver and cook it only 2–3 minutes, stirring.
  4. Salt and pepper at the end, add the parsley and serve immediately.
Chef's tip. Do not overcook the liver and salt it only at the end, otherwise it hardens. Serve with polenta.
Did you know? The Greeks and Romans already served liver with a sweet ingredient, often figs; in Venice onions are used, stewed for a long time until they turn sweet.

Allergens: Milk
