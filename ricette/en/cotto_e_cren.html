Journey city: Trieste

Cotto e cren

Servings4
Prep15 min
Cook10 min
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Trieste boiled ham (baked in crust) · 400 g
  • Fresh horseradish root · 100 g
  • White wine vinegar · 1 tablespoon
  • Sugar · 1 pinch
  • Mustard (optional) · to taste
  • Bread (kaiser or francesino roll) · 4 rolls

Step by step

  1. Warm the ham whole: steamed or in the oven at 150 °C for 10 minutes, wrapped in foil.
  2. Peel and finely grate the horseradish root (it is very pungent, careful with your eyes).
  3. Dress the horseradish with vinegar and a pinch of sugar.
  4. Cut the ham into thick slices, by hand, and serve it warm with horseradish, mustard and bread.
Chef's tip. Fresh horseradish loses its strength quickly: grate it at the last moment.
Did you know? It is the signature dish of the Trieste 'buffet' taverns, born in the days of the Austro-Hungarian Empire.

Allergens: Gluten, Mustard, Sulphites
