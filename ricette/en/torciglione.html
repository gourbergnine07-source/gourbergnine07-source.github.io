Torciglione

Torciglione is Umbria's snake-shaped Christmas cake: tradition says it recalls the eels of Lake Trasimeno.

Servings8
Prep30 min
Cook40 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Tree nuts, Eggs, Gluten

Ingredients

  • Blanched almonds · 300 g
  • Sugar · 200 g
  • Egg whites · 3
  • Flour · 30 g
  • Lemon zest · zest of 1
  • Pine nuts · 50 g
  • Coffee beans · 2
  • Candied cherry · 1

Step by step

  1. Finely grind the almonds with the sugar, keeping a handful whole for decoration.
  2. Whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks and gently fold them into the ground almonds with the flour and lemon zest, until you have a soft dough.
  3. On a lined baking tray, shape the dough into a coiled snake. Cover it with pine nuts and almonds, add two coffee beans for the eyes and the candied cherry for the tongue.
  4. Bake at 170 °C for about 40 minutes, until golden outside and still soft inside.
Chef's tip. Brush the surface with a little egg white before adding the pine nuts: they stick and turn glossy in the oven.
