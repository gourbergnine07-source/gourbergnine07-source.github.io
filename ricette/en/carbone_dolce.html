Sweet coal

On the night between January 5 and 6, the Befana fills the stockings: sweets for good children and coal for those who misbehaved. Today that coal is almost always made of sugar.

Servings10
Prep20 min
Cook15 min
Rest2 h
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Eggs

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Sugar · 500 g
  • Water · 100 ml
  • Egg whites · 1
  • Icing sugar · 100 g
  • Black food colouring · 1 tsp

Step by step

  1. In a bowl, mix the egg white with the icing sugar and black colouring into a thick icing.
  2. Together with an adult, cook the sugar and water until the syrup is thick and just starting to colour, about 15 minutes.
  3. Take it off the heat, add the black icing straight away and stir hard: the sugar will puff up. Pour it into a mould lined with baking paper.
  4. Let it cool for a couple of hours, then break it into irregular chunks, like real lumps of coal.
Chef's tip. Boiling sugar burns badly: keep children away from the stove and let them decorate the bags instead.
