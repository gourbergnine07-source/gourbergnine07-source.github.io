Struffoli

In Naples it isn't Christmas without struffoli: little balls of fried dough covered in honey and «diavolilli», the coloured sprinkles, made in big batches and given to family and friends.

Servings8
Prep1 h
Cook20 min
Rest30 min
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Ingredients

  • Flour · 400 g
  • Eggs · 4
  • Sugar · 1 tbsp
  • Butter · 50 g
  • Anise liqueur or limoncello · 1 tbsp
  • Lemon zest · zest of 1
  • Vegetable oil for frying · to taste
  • Honey · 300 g
  • Candied orange and citron peel · 80 g
  • Coloured sprinkles (diavolilli) · 2 tbsp

Step by step

  1. Knead the flour, eggs, sugar, butter, liqueur and lemon zest into a smooth dough; let it rest, covered, for 30 minutes.
  2. Roll the dough into finger-thick ropes and cut them into pieces of about 1 cm.
  3. Fry the little balls a few at a time in hot oil for 2–3 minutes per batch (about 20 minutes in all) until golden, then drain on kitchen paper.
  4. Warm the honey in a wide pan, add the struffoli and stir until they're all glossy. Arrange them in a ring and decorate with candied peel and coloured sprinkles.
Chef's tip. Struffoli can be made a few days ahead: covered, they stay soft and the honey makes them even tastier.
