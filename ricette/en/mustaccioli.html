Mustaccioli

The name mustaccioli comes from the Latin «mustaceum», a cake made with grape must: today the must has almost disappeared, but the diamond shape with its chocolate glaze has stayed.

Servings12
Prep40 min
Cook15 min
Rest1 h
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Gluten

Ingredients

  • Flour · 500 g
  • Sugar · 200 g
  • Honey · 150 g
  • Unsweetened cocoa · 20 g
  • Pisto spice mix (cinnamon, cloves, nutmeg, star anise) · 1 tsp
  • Orange zest · zest of 1
  • Water · 150 ml
  • Dark chocolate · 300 g

Step by step

  1. Knead the flour, sugar, honey, cocoa, pisto and orange zest with the warm water into a smooth dough; let it rest for an hour.
  2. Roll the dough out about 1 cm thick and cut it into diamonds.
  3. Bake on baking paper at 180 °C for about 15 minutes and let them cool.
  4. Melt the chocolate in a bain-marie, dip the mustaccioli and let them set on a rack.
Chef's tip. For a glossier coating, add a teaspoon of vegetable oil to the melted chocolate.
