Pinoccate

Pinoccate are Perugia's Christmas sweets: white or cocoa, diamond-shaped, and given as gifts wrapped in coloured paper.

Servings8
Prep30 min
Cook10 min
Rest1 h
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Tree nuts

Ingredients

  • Sugar · 300 g
  • Water · 100 ml
  • Pine nuts · 200 g
  • Lemon zest · zest of 1
  • Unsweetened cocoa · 20 g

Step by step

  1. Cook the sugar and water over medium heat without stirring until the syrup is thick and forms a thread, about 10 minutes.
  2. Take it off the heat, add the pine nuts and lemon zest and stir until the sugar turns opaque. For the dark pinoccate, add the cocoa to half the mixture.
  3. Spread the still-warm mixture between two sheets of baking paper, about 1 cm thick, and cut it into diamonds while lukewarm.
  4. Let them harden for an hour, then wrap the pinoccate one by one in coloured paper.
Chef's tip. Work quickly: the sugar sets in a few minutes. If it gets too hard, put it back on the heat for a moment.
