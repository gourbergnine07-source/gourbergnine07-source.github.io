Befanini

Servings8
Prep30 min
Cook15 min
Rest30 min
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Flour · 500 g
  • Sugar · 200 g
  • Soft butter · 100 g
  • Eggs · 3
  • Baking powder · 8 g
  • Orange zest · zest of 1
  • Rum · 2 tbsp
  • Milk · to taste
  • Coloured sprinkles (diavolilli) · 3 tbsp

Step by step

  1. Knead the flour, sugar, butter, eggs, baking powder, orange zest and rum; if the dough is too stiff, add a splash of milk. Let it rest in the fridge for 30 minutes.
  2. Roll the dough out half a centimetre thick and cut out stars, hearts, little horses and Befana shapes.
  3. Brush the biscuits with a little egg or milk and cover them with coloured sprinkles.
  4. Bake at 180 °C for 12–15 minutes, until just golden underneath.
Chef's tip. It's a perfect recipe to make with children: they choose the shapes and add the sprinkles.
Did you know? In Versilia and around Lucca, befanini are baked for the night of the Befana, in shapes of stars, hearts and animals covered in coloured sprinkles.

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk
