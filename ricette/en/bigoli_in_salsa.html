Bigoli in salsa

Servings4
Prep15 min
Cook47 min
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Bigoli (or thick whole-wheat spaghetti) · 400 g
  • Salt-packed anchovies · 100 g
  • White onions · 400 g
  • Extra-virgin olive oil · 8 tablespoons
  • Chopped parsley · 1 tablespoon
  • Pepper · to taste

Step by step

  1. Slice the onions thin and stew them with the oil and a few spoonfuls of water, covered, over low heat for 30 minutes.
  2. Desalt and bone the anchovies, then add them to the onions and let them melt in, stirring.
  3. Cook the bigoli in lightly salted water.
  4. Drain and toss them in the sauce with a bit of cooking water. Finish with parsley and pepper.
Chef's tip. No Parmesan needed: the flavor comes from the anchovies and onion.
Did you know? It is the traditional dish of lean days, like Christmas Eve and Good Friday.

Allergens: Gluten, Fish
