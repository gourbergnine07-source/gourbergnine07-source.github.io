Mondeghili (Milanese meatballs)

Mondeghili have been a De.Co. of the City of Milan since 2008; the name probably comes from the Spanish «albóndiga», meatball.

Servings4
Prep20 min
Cook8 min
Rest10 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Ingredients

  • Leftover boiled beef · 300 g
  • Mortadella · 100 g
  • Stale bread, crustless · 80 g
  • Milk · 100 ml
  • Eggs · 2
  • Grated Grana Padano or Parmigiano Reggiano · 50 g
  • Chopped parsley · 1 tbsp
  • Grated zest of an untreated lemon · 1/2
  • Breadcrumbs · 80 g
  • Butter · 60 g
  • Salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Soak the bread in the milk for 10 minutes, then squeeze it well.
  2. Mince the boiled beef and the mortadella and mix them with the bread, eggs, cheese, parsley and lemon zest; season with salt.
  3. Shape slightly flattened meatballs the size of a walnut and coat them in breadcrumbs.
  4. Fry them in hot butter for about 8 minutes, 4 per side, until golden. Serve hot.
Chef's tip. They were born to use up leftover boiled meat: leftover roast works too.
