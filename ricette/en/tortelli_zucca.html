Pumpkin tortelli

In Mantua, pumpkin tortelli are the dish of Christmas Eve dinner; their sweet-and-spicy filling is traced back to the Renaissance cooking of the Gonzaga court.

Servings4
Prep1 h
Cook50 min
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk, Tree nuts, Mustard

Ingredients

  • Flour · 400 g
  • Eggs · 4
  • Pumpkin (firm and sweet) · 1 kg
  • Amaretti biscuits · 100 g
  • Apple mostarda · 100 g
  • Grated Parmigiano · 100 g
  • Nutmeg · 1 pinch
  • Butter · 80 g
  • Sage · 6 leaves

Step by step

  1. Bake the pumpkin in slices at 180 °C for about 45 minutes until soft, then push it through a sieve.
  2. Mix in the crumbled amaretti, chopped mostarda, Parmigiano and nutmeg. The filling tastes better if it rests overnight in the fridge.
  3. Knead the flour and eggs, roll out a thin sheet and cut it into rectangles. Put a teaspoon of filling in the middle, fold over and seal the edges well.
  4. Cook the tortelli in salted water for 4–5 minutes and dress them with melted butter, sage and Parmigiano.
Chef's tip. Choose a firm, sweet pumpkin: if the filling is too wet, dry the pulp in a pan for a few minutes before seasoning it.
