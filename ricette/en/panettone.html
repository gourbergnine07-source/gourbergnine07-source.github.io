Panettone

A legend tells that at the Sforza court the Christmas cake got burnt and a kitchen boy, Toni, made a new one with butter, eggs and raisins: «el pan de Toni», which gave panettone its name.

Servings10
Prep1 h
Cook50 min
Rest19 h
DifficultyHard

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Ingredients

  • Strong bread flour · 500 g
  • Refreshed sourdough starter · 150 g
  • Sugar · 150 g
  • Egg yolks · 6
  • Soft butter · 180 g
  • Honey · 1 tbsp
  • Raisins · 150 g
  • Candied orange and citron peel · 100 g
  • Orange zest · zest of 1
  • Salt · 5 g

Step by step

  1. First dough: knead half the flour with the starter, half the sugar, 3 yolks and 80 g of butter. Cover and let it rise until tripled, about 12 hours.
  2. Second dough: add the rest of the flour, sugar and yolks, the honey and the salt, then the butter a little at a time. Finally fold in the raisins, candied peel and orange zest.
  3. Put the dough in the paper mould and let it rise until it reaches the rim, 6–8 hours. Score a cross on the dome and place a knob of butter in the middle.
  4. Bake at 170 °C for about 50 minutes. As soon as it comes out, skewer the base with two long skewers and hang the panettone upside down overnight so it doesn't collapse.
Chef's tip. If you don't have a sourdough starter, many home recipes use fresh yeast instead: the flavour changes a little, but the panettone still turns out well.
