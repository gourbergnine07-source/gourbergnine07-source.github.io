Boiled meats with mostarda

Servings6
Prep20 min
Cook3 h
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Beef (brisket or shank) · 1 kg
  • Hen or capon · 1/2
  • Cotechino sausage · 1
  • Onion · 1
  • Carrots · 2
  • Celery · 1 stalk
  • Coarse salt · to taste
  • Cremona mostarda · 200 g

Step by step

  1. In a large pot, bring the water to the boil with the onion, carrots, celery and coarse salt.
  2. Add the beef once the water is boiling, so the meat keeps its flavour, and simmer for about 3 hours. Add the hen halfway through.
  3. Cook the cotechino separately in another pot, as it is very fatty: about 2 and a half hours over low heat (or the time on the pack, if it's pre-cooked).
  4. Serve the meats sliced and piping hot, with Cremona mostarda and a ladle of broth.
Chef's tip. Don't throw away the broth: strained, it's perfect for cooking tortellini or anolini the next day.
Did you know? Cremona mostarda is whole candied fruit, such as cherries, pears and mandarins, in a syrup flavoured with mustard essence: sweet and hot at once, it's the classic partner of the festive boiled meats.

Allergens: Celery, Mustard, Sulphites
