Cremona nougat

In Cremona they say torrone was first served at the wedding of Bianca Maria Visconti and Francesco Sforza in 1441, shaped like the Torrazzo, the city's bell tower.

Servings12
Prep30 min
Cook1 h 10 min
Rest4 h
DifficultyHard

Allergens: Tree nuts, Eggs

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Honey · 250 g
  • Sugar · 250 g
  • Egg whites · 2
  • Blanched almonds · 400 g
  • Edible wafer paper · 2
  • Lemon zest · zest of 1

Step by step

  1. Toast the almonds in the oven at 160 °C for 10 minutes and keep them warm.
  2. Warm the honey in a bain-marie and stir for about an hour, until it turns pale and thick. Meanwhile whisk the egg whites to stiff peaks and fold them into the honey.
  3. Cook the sugar with a splash of water into a thick syrup and pour it slowly into the honey, stirring all the time. Then add the warm almonds and the lemon zest.
  4. Spread the mixture between two sheets of wafer paper in a rectangular tin, press it down well and let it cool for a few hours before cutting it into bars.
Chef's tip. Try swapping some of the almonds for hazelnuts or pistachios, or coat the bars in dark chocolate.
