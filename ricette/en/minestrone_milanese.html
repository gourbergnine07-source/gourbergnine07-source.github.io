Minestrone alla milanese (vegetable soup with rice)

Minestrone alla milanese is made with rice: in winter it was eaten hot, in summer cold or lukewarm.

Servings6
Prep25 min
Cook1 h 25 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Celery, Milk

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Cooked borlotti beans · 250 g
  • Savoy cabbage · 250 g
  • Potatoes · 2
  • Carrots · 2
  • Courgettes · 2
  • Celery · 1 stalk
  • Onion · 1
  • Pancetta · 50 g
  • Rice · 150 g
  • Grated Grana Padano or Parmigiano Reggiano · 50 g
  • Salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. In a large pot, sauté the chopped onion and pancetta for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the diced vegetables and the beans, cover with 2 litres of salted water and cook over low heat for 1 hour.
  3. Add the rice and cook for 20 more minutes, stirring now and then.
  4. Serve hot or lukewarm, with grated cheese and a drizzle of oil.
Chef's tip. To serve it cold in summer, as the Milanese used to, ladle it into bowls and let it cool.
