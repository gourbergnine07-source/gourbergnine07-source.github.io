Friggione

It is one of the oldest side dishes of Bolognese cooking: once it simmered for hours on the home stove.

Servings4
Prep20 min
Cook2 h 30 min
Rest1 h
DifficultyEasy

Allergens:

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • White onions · 1 kg
  • Peeled tomatoes · 400 g
  • Extra-virgin olive oil (or lard) · 4 tablespoons
  • Sugar · 1 teaspoon
  • Salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Slice the onions very thin, salt them and let rest 1 hour so they lose their water.
  2. Stew them in a covered pan with the oil, over very low heat, for about 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
  3. Add the crushed tomatoes and the sugar.
  4. Cook uncovered another 1–1.5 hours, until it becomes a soft red-brown cream.
Chef's tip. Excellent as a side for boiled meats or spread on bread and crescentine.
