Trentino Zelten

Zelten is THE Trentino Christmas cake: the name comes from the German "selten", "rare", because it was once baked only for special occasions.

Servings8
Prep40 min
Cook30 min
Rest8 h
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Gluten, Tree nuts, Eggs, Milk

Ingredients

  • Raisins · 75 g
  • Dried figs · 125 g
  • Candied fruit (citron and orange) · 50 g
  • Walnuts · 75 g
  • Almonds · 50 g
  • Pine nuts · 50 g
  • Milk · 200 ml
  • Rum · 1 glass
  • Flour · 250 g
  • Soft butter · 75 g
  • Sugar · 75 g
  • Eggs · 2
  • Baking powder · 1 sachet
  • Cinnamon and vanilla · to taste

Step by step

  1. The night before, soak the raisins, figs and candied fruit in the milk with the rum.
  2. Add the eggs, the soft butter, a pinch of salt, the flour, the spices and the baking powder: the dough must stay soft.
  3. Pour into a greased pan, brush with egg white and decorate as you like with walnuts, almonds and candied fruit.
  4. Bake in a preheated oven at 175 °C.
Chef's tip. Every Trentino family has its own variant: try rye flour, or soak the fruit in grappa instead.
