Rinforzo salad

It's called «rinforzo» (reinforcement) because during the holidays new ingredients were added every day to «reinforce» it, from Christmas Eve until Epiphany.

Servings6
Prep20 min
Cook10 min
Rest1 h
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Fish, Sulphites

Ingredients

  • Cauliflower · 1
  • Gaeta black olives · 100 g
  • Pickled papaccelle peppers · 150 g
  • Giardiniera (pickled vegetables) · 150 g
  • Salted anchovies · 6
  • Capers · 1 tbsp
  • Extra virgin olive oil · 5 tbsp
  • Wine vinegar · 2 tbsp
  • Salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Break the cauliflower into florets and boil in salted water for about 10 minutes: it should stay crunchy.
  2. Rinse and clean the anchovies, cut the peppers into pieces and drain the giardiniera.
  3. In a large bowl, combine the cold cauliflower with the olives, peppers, giardiniera, anchovies and capers.
  4. Dress with oil, vinegar and a little salt, toss and let the flavours blend for at least an hour before serving.
Chef's tip. Dress the cauliflower only once it's cold, so it stays firm and doesn't crumble.
