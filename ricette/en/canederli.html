Canederli in brodo

They are the signature dish of Trentino and Alto Adige, born to avoid wasting stale bread.

Servings4
Prep30 min
Cook20 min
Rest30 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk, Celery

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Stale bread, cubed · 300 g
  • Speck, cubed · 150 g
  • Milk · 250 ml
  • Eggs · 3
  • Flour · 50 g
  • Onion · 1/2
  • Butter · 20 g
  • Chives or parsley · 1 tablespoon
  • Nutmeg and salt · to taste
  • Meat broth · 1.5 liters

Step by step

  1. Sauté the chopped onion and speck in the butter for 5 minutes.
  2. Beat the eggs with the milk and pour over the bread. Let rest 30 minutes.
  3. Add the speck, onion, herbs, flour, nutmeg and salt and knead by hand.
  4. With wet hands, shape balls of 5–6 cm, pressing them well.
  5. Cook them in salted boiling water for 15 minutes, then serve them in the hot broth.
Chef's tip. They can also be served dry, with melted butter and grated cheese.
