Cotechino with lentils

Modena's cotechino and zampone are PGI products; one tradition says zampone was invented in Mirandola in 1511, during the siege by Pope Julius II's troops.

Servings6
Prep20 min
Cook2 h 30 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Celery, Milk

Ingredients

  • Cotechino sausage · 1
  • Lentils · 300 g
  • Onion · 1
  • Carrots · 1
  • Celery · 1 stalk
  • Extra virgin olive oil · 3 tbsp
  • Bay leaf · 1 leaf
  • Meat broth · 1 litre
  • Potatoes · 600 g
  • Milk · 150 ml
  • Butter · 30 g

Step by step

  1. Prick the cotechino with a toothpick and simmer it from cold water for about 2 and a half hours; if it's pre-cooked, follow the time on the pack.
  2. In a pot, gently fry the chopped onion, carrot and celery in the oil, add the lentils and bay leaf and cover with hot broth. Cook for about 40 minutes, adding broth if needed.
  3. Boil the potatoes, mash them and stir over the heat with hot milk and butter until you have a soft purée.
  4. Cut the cotechino into thick slices and serve it piping hot on the lentils, with the purée alongside.
Chef's tip. Small lentils, like those from Castelluccio, don't need soaking and keep their shape when cooked.
