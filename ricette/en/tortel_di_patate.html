Tortel di patate

Servings4
Prep20 min
Cook20 min
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Potatoes · 1 kg
  • Onion (optional) · 1/2
  • Flour · 2 tablespoons
  • Salt · to taste
  • Seed oil for frying · to taste

Step by step

  1. Peel the potatoes and grate them coarsely.
  2. Squeeze them very well in a cloth to remove the water.
  3. Add salt, flour and, if you like, the very finely chopped onion.
  4. In a pan with plenty of hot oil, spread the dough into thin fritters of 15–20 cm.
  5. Fry 5 minutes per side, until golden and crispy. Drain on paper. In batches: about 20 minutes in all.
Chef's tip. They are served with cold cuts, cheeses, sauerkraut or bean salad.
Did you know? It is a 'cake' of only potatoes, born in the Trentino valleys where potatoes were everyday food.

Allergens: Gluten
