Tiramisù

Servings6
Prep30 min
Rest4 h
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Mascarpone · 500 g
  • Pasteurized eggs · 4
  • Sugar · 100 g
  • Savoiardi (ladyfingers) · 300 g
  • Cold espresso coffee · 300 ml
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder · to taste

Step by step

  1. Separate the yolks from the whites.
  2. Whip the yolks with the sugar until pale and fluffy, then add the mascarpone and mix into a smooth cream.
  3. Whip the whites to firm peaks and fold them into the cream gently, from bottom to top.
  4. Quickly dip the savoiardi in the coffee and arrange them in a dish.
  5. Alternate layers of savoiardi and cream, finishing with the cream.
  6. Cover and refrigerate at least 4 hours. Before serving, dust with cocoa.
Chef's tip. The eggs are raw: always use pasteurized eggs, especially if serving to children, the elderly or pregnant women.

Allergens: Eggs, Milk, Gluten
