Tortellini in brodo

According to a legend, an innkeeper spied on Venus in an inn and created the tortellino imitating her navel.

Servings4
Prep2 h
Cook3 h 13 min
Rest2 h 30 min
DifficultyHard

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk, Celery

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Type 00 flour (pasta sheet) · 300 g
  • Eggs (pasta sheet) · 3
  • Pork loin · 100 g
  • Prosciutto crudo · 100 g
  • Mortadella di Bologna · 100 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano · 150 g
  • Egg (filling) · 1
  • Butter · 20 g
  • Nutmeg · 1 pinch
  • Meat broth (beef and capon) · 2 liters

Step by step

  1. Broth: cook the beef and capon in cold water with carrot, celery and onion for about 3 hours over low heat, then strain.
  2. Filling: brown the pork loin pieces in butter, then chop very finely with prosciutto and mortadella.
  3. Add Parmigiano, egg and nutmeg. Let the filling rest in the fridge at least 2 hours (better overnight).
  4. Pasta sheet: knead flour and eggs, rest 30 minutes and roll out a very thin sheet.
  5. Cut squares of about 3 cm, put a small walnut-sized ball of filling in the center and fold into a triangle.
  6. Wrap the triangle around your fingertip and join the two points, pressing well.
  7. Cook the tortellini in boiling broth: they are ready 2–3 minutes after they float to the surface.
Chef's tip. In Bologna, tortellini are eaten only in broth, especially at Christmas.
