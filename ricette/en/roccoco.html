Roccocò

Servings12
Prep30 min
Cook25 min
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Flour · 500 g
  • Sugar · 400 g
  • Whole almonds · 300 g
  • Candied orange and citron peel · 100 g
  • Pisto spice mix (cinnamon, cloves, nutmeg, star anise) · 1 tsp
  • Orange zest · zest of 1
  • Water · 150 ml
  • Egg (for brushing) · 1

Step by step

  1. Toast the almonds in the oven at 180 °C for 5 minutes.
  2. Mix the flour, sugar, pisto, orange zest, chopped candied peel and almonds with the water into a firm dough.
  3. Shape ropes two fingers thick and close them into rings about 10 cm across. Place them on baking paper and brush with beaten egg.
  4. Bake at 180 °C for about 20 minutes, until nicely golden. They harden as they cool: that's how they should be.
Chef's tip. Roccocò are very hard: in Naples people dunk them in wine, Marsala or vermouth.
Did you know? Roccocò get their name from their round, curly shape, like rococo decorations. In Naples people start making them for the Immaculate Conception on December 8, together with mustaccioli.

Allergens: Gluten, Tree nuts, Eggs
