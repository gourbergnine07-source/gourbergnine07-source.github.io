Cotoletta alla milanese (breaded veal chop)

Cotoletta alla milanese has been a De.Co. of the City of Milan since 2008: with the bone, cooked in butter.

Servings4
Prep15 min
Cook15 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk

Ingredients

  • Bone-in veal chops, about 2 cm thick · 4
  • Eggs · 2
  • Breadcrumbs · 150 g
  • Clarified butter · 150 g
  • Salt · to taste
  • Lemon (to serve) · 1

Step by step

  1. Pound the meat lightly, keeping the bone, then nick the edge in two or three places so it doesn't curl.
  2. Dip each chop in the beaten egg, then in the breadcrumbs, pressing well with your hands.
  3. Fry the chops in hot clarified butter for about 15 minutes, 7-8 per side, until golden.
  4. Drain on paper towels, salt them and serve with a lemon wedge.
Chef's tip. The butter shouldn't sizzle hard: the cutlet cooks gently and stays tender inside. Salt only at the end.
