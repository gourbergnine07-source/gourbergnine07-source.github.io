Journey city: Trieste

Presnitz

Servings8
Prep40 min
Cook35 min
Rest30 min
DifficultyMedium

Ingredients

  • Ready-rolled puff pastry · 250 g
  • Walnuts · 100 g
  • Almonds · 50 g
  • Hazelnuts · 50 g
  • Pine nuts · 30 g
  • Raisins · 80 g
  • Breadcrumbs · 40 g
  • Butter · 20 g
  • Sugar · 60 g
  • Dark chocolate · 30 g
  • Candied fruit · 30 g
  • Lemon and orange zest · 1 each
  • Rum · 3 tablespoons
  • Cinnamon · 1 pinch
  • Egg yolk for brushing · 1

Step by step

  1. Soak the raisins in the rum for 30 minutes.
  2. Toast the breadcrumbs in the butter. Roughly chop the walnuts, almonds, hazelnuts and chocolate.
  3. Combine the nuts, pine nuts, rum-soaked raisins, breadcrumbs, sugar, candied fruit, zests and cinnamon.
  4. Roll out the puff pastry into a strip about 10 cm wide. Lay the filling in a cylinder and roll the pastry around it.
  5. Shape the roll into a spiral, brush with the yolk.
  6. Bake at 180 °C for 30–35 minutes, until golden.
Chef's tip. It is the Easter sweet of Trieste, but you'll find it in pastry shops all year round.
Did you know? Legend says it was created for Empress Sissi's visit, and the name would come from 'Preis-Prinzessin'.

Allergens: Gluten, Tree nuts, Eggs, Milk
