Pandoro

Servings10
Prep1 h
Cook45 min
Rest18 h 30 min
DifficultyHard

Ingredients

  • Strong bread flour · 500 g
  • Fresh yeast · 15 g
  • Sugar · 150 g
  • Eggs · 4
  • Egg yolks · 2
  • Soft butter · 200 g
  • Milk · 80 ml
  • Vanilla · 1 pod
  • Salt · 5 g
  • Icing sugar · to taste

Step by step

  1. Mix 100 g of flour with the yeast, warm milk and a spoonful of sugar; let it rise until doubled, about 1 hour.
  2. Add the rest of the flour, the sugar, eggs, yolks, vanilla and salt and knead well; work in half the butter a little at a time and let it rise in the fridge overnight.
  3. Roll out the dough, put the remaining butter on top and fold it like puff pastry, three times, with 30 minutes in the fridge between folds. Then shape a ball and put it in the buttered star mould.
  4. Let it rise until it nearly reaches the rim of the mould, about 4 hours.
  5. Bake at 170 °C for about 45 minutes. Unmould when cold and dust with icing sugar.
Chef's tip. Before serving, put the pandoro in a bag with the icing sugar and shake it: it gets evenly coated all over.
Did you know? Pandoro was born in Verona: on October 14, 1894, pastry chef Domenico Melegatti registered the recipe, and the eight-pointed star mould was designed by the painter Angelo Dall'Oca Bianca.

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk
