Pappa al pomodoro

It was born to avoid wasting stale bread and overripe tomatoes: a poor dish that became the symbol of Firenze.

Servings4
Prep15 min
Cook37 min
Rest15 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Gluten, Celery

Ingredients

  • Stale Tuscan bread · 300 g
  • Ripe tomatoes (or peeled) · 800 g
  • Garlic · 2 cloves
  • Basil · 1 small bunch
  • Hot vegetable broth · 750 ml
  • Extra-virgin olive oil · 6 tablespoons
  • Salt and pepper · to taste

Step by step

  1. Cut the stale bread into slices and then into pieces.
  2. In a pot, heat 4 tablespoons of oil and let the crushed garlic infuse for 1–2 minutes, without burning it.
  3. Add the broken-up tomatoes, salt and cook for 15 minutes.
  4. Add the bread and hot broth. Cook over low heat for 15–20 minutes, stirring often, until the bread falls apart into a thick cream.
  5. Turn off the heat, add the basil torn by hand and let rest at least 15 minutes.
  6. Serve warm or at room temperature, with a drizzle of raw oil and a grind of pepper.
Chef's tip. It is even better the day after. In summer it is also eaten cold.
