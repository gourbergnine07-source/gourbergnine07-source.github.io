Risotto alla milanese (saffron risotto)

Legend says the yellow risotto was born in 1574 on the Duomo building site: an assistant of the glass master Valerio di Fiandra, nicknamed «Saffron», coloured the rice at a wedding feast.

Servings4
Prep10 min
Cook25 min
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Milk, Sulphites, Celery

Ingredients

  • Carnaroli or Vialone Nano rice · 320 g
  • Meat stock · 1.2 l
  • Saffron threads or powder · 0.2 g
  • Butter · 60 g
  • Beef marrow (optional) · 30 g
  • Small onion · 1
  • Dry white wine · 100 ml
  • Grated Grana Padano or Parmigiano Reggiano · 50 g
  • Salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Heat the stock and keep it hot on the stove. Dissolve the saffron in a small ladle of hot stock.
  2. In a wide pan, gently soften the chopped onion and the marrow in half the butter over low heat for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the rice and toast it for 2 minutes, stirring, then pour in the wine and let it evaporate.
  4. Cook the rice for about 18 minutes, adding the stock one ladle at a time and stirring often; halfway through, add the saffron.
  5. Take off the heat and stir in the remaining butter and the cheese (the «mantecatura»). Cover for a moment and serve «all'onda», soft and creamy.
Chef's tip. Use a light stock and keep it hot: cold stock stops the rice from cooking.
