Strudel di mele

The strudel arrived from Vienna, but the very thin dough recalls Ottoman sweets like baklava.

Servings8
Prep50 min
Cook50 min
Rest30 min
DifficultyHard

Allergens: Gluten, Milk, Tree nuts

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Type 00 flour (dough) · 250 g
  • Lukewarm water (dough) · 120 ml
  • Seed oil (dough) · 2 tablespoons
  • Salt · 1 pinch
  • Renetta apples · 1 kg
  • Sugar · 80 g
  • Raisins · 50 g
  • Pine nuts · 40 g
  • Breadcrumbs · 60 g
  • Butter · 90 g
  • Cinnamon · 1 teaspoon
  • Lemon zest · 1
  • Icing sugar · to taste

Step by step

  1. Knead flour, water, oil and salt for 10 minutes into a smooth, elastic dough.
  2. Oil it, cover and rest 30 minutes in a warm place.
  3. Peel the apples, cut into thin slices and mix with sugar, cinnamon, raisins, pine nuts and lemon zest.
  4. Toast the breadcrumbs in 40 g of butter.
  5. On a floured cloth, pull the dough with your hands, from the center outwards, until very thin (you must see through it).
  6. Brush with 50 g of melted butter, sprinkle the breadcrumbs and lay the apples on a third of the dough.
  7. Roll it up with the help of the cloth, place the strudel in a tray and brush with butter.
  8. Bake at 180 °C for 40–45 minutes. Serve warm dusted with icing sugar.
Chef's tip. If you don't want to stretch the dough by hand, you can use puff pastry: the result is different, but excellent.
