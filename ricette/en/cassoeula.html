Cassoeula (pork and savoy cabbage stew)

Servings6
Prep30 min
Cook2 h 40 min
DifficultyMedium

Ingredients

  • Pork ribs · 800 g
  • Pork rind · 200 g
  • Verzini (Milanese sausages) or sausages · 300 g
  • Savoy cabbage · 1.2 kg
  • Onion · 1
  • Carrot · 1
  • Celery · 1 stalk
  • Butter · 30 g
  • Tomato passata · 2 tbsp
  • Dry white wine · 100 ml
  • Meat stock · 500 ml
  • Salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Boil the pork rind in water for 30 minutes, then drain and cut it into strips.
  2. In a large pot, sauté the chopped onion, carrot and celery in the butter, then add the ribs and rind and brown them for 10 minutes.
  3. Pour in the wine, add the passata and stock, cover and cook over low heat for 1 hour.
  4. Wash the cabbage, separate the leaves and remove the toughest ribs.
  5. Add the cabbage and the verzini, cover and cook for 1 more hour, until the cabbage is soft and the sauce is thick. Serve with polenta.
Chef's tip. It's even better the next day, gently reheated: the flavours blend.
Did you know? It was the dish of Saint Anthony the Abbot, 17 January, which marked the end of the pig-slaughtering season; and after the first frosts the cabbage becomes more tender and sweet.

Allergens: Celery, Milk, Sulphites
