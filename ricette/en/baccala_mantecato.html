Baccalà mantecato

In Venezia they call it 'baccalà' but it is stockfish: the merchant Pietro Querini, shipwrecked in the Lofoten Islands in 1432, brought it back.

Servings6
Prep40 min
Cook20 min
Rest72 h
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Fish

Ingredients

  • Dried stockfish · 300 g
  • Delicate extra-virgin olive oil · 250 ml
  • Garlic · 1 clove
  • Bay leaf · 1 leaf
  • Chopped parsley · 1 tablespoon
  • Salt and pepper · to taste
  • Toasted polenta or crostini · to serve

Step by step

  1. Soak the stockfish in cold water for 3 days, changing the water every 8 hours.
  2. Put it in a pot with cold water and bay leaf, bring to a boil and cook 20 minutes.
  3. Drain, remove skin and bones and flake it.
  4. Beat it vigorously with a wooden spoon or a whisk, pouring in the oil in a thin stream as for a mayonnaise, for 15–20 minutes, until it becomes a white, fluffy cream.
  5. Add the very finely chopped garlic, parsley, salt and pepper.
  6. Serve on slices of toasted polenta or crostini.
Chef's tip. You'll find it in Venetian bacari as a 'cicchetto', with a small glass of wine.
