Journey city: Trieste

Goulash triestino

Servings4
Prep20 min
Cook3 h
DifficultyMedium

Ingredients

  • Beef (shoulder or shank), cubed · 800 g
  • Onions · 600 g
  • Sweet paprika · 2 tablespoons
  • Hot paprika · 1/2 teaspoon
  • Tomato paste · 1 tablespoon
  • Garlic · 1 clove
  • Cumin, marjoram, bay leaf · to taste
  • Lard or oil · 3 tablespoons
  • Broth or water · 500 ml
  • Salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Slice the onions and stew them in the lard over low heat for 20 minutes, until soft and golden.
  2. Take the pot off the heat and add the paprika, stirring: it must not burn.
  3. Put it back on the heat, add the meat and brown it well.
  4. Add tomato paste, garlic, spices and broth.
  5. Cover and cook over low heat for 2–2.5 hours, until the meat is very tender and the sauce thick.
Chef's tip. Serve it with polenta or potatoes, the classic sides in Trieste.
Did you know? It arrived from Hungary with the Austro-Hungarian Empire and became a home dish in Trieste.

Allergens: Celery
