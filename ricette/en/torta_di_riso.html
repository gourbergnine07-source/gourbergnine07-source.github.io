Torta di riso

It is also called 'torta degli addobbi': it was made for the parish Addobbi festival of Bologna.

Servings8
Prep20 min
Cook1 h 40 min
Rest12 h
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Milk, Eggs, Tree nuts, Gluten

Ingredients

  • Whole milk · 1 liter
  • Originario rice · 200 g
  • Sugar · 250 g
  • Eggs · 5
  • Peeled almonds, chopped · 100 g
  • Candied citron, diced · 50 g
  • Lemon zest · 1
  • Almond liqueur · 30 ml
  • Butter and breadcrumbs for the pan · to taste

Step by step

  1. Cook the rice in the milk with the lemon zest and half the sugar over low heat for about 40 minutes, until the milk is absorbed. Let cool.
  2. Remove the zest and add the eggs one at a time, then the remaining sugar, the almonds and the citron.
  3. Pour into a 24 cm buttered pan dusted with breadcrumbs.
  4. Bake at 170 °C for about 1 hour.
  5. Fresh out of the oven, moisten it with the liqueur. Serve it the next day, cut into squares.
Chef's tip. It keeps well for a few days and improves with rest.
