Nadalin

Nadalin, a low star-shaped cake, is considered the ancestor of pandoro: tradition says it was born in Verona in the 13th century, for the Christmas of the della Scala lords.

Servings8
Prep30 min
Cook30 min
Rest4 h
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk, Tree nuts

Ingredients

  • Flour · 400 g
  • Fresh yeast · 15 g
  • Sugar · 100 g
  • Soft butter · 120 g
  • Eggs · 2
  • Milk · 80 ml
  • Lemon zest · zest of 1
  • Pine nuts · 30 g
  • Pearl sugar · 2 tbsp

Step by step

  1. Dissolve the yeast in the warm milk and knead with the flour, sugar, eggs and lemon zest.
  2. Add the butter a little at a time until the dough is smooth and elastic, then let it rise, covered, until doubled, about 3 hours.
  3. Spread the dough a couple of centimetres thick in a buttered star-shaped mould and let it rise for another hour.
  4. Sprinkle with pine nuts and pearl sugar and bake at 180 °C for about 30 minutes, until golden.
Chef's tip. If you don't have a star mould, use a shallow cake tin and score a star with a knife before baking.
