Orzetto alla trentina

It is the soup of cold mountain days, made in Trentino for centuries.

Servings4
Prep20 min
Cook1 h 10 min
Rest10 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Gluten, Celery

Ingredients

  • Pearl barley · 250 g
  • Speck, cubed · 100 g
  • Potatoes · 2
  • Carrots · 2
  • Celery · 1 stalk
  • Onion · 1
  • Leek (optional) · 1
  • Meat or vegetable broth · 2 liters
  • Oil or butter · 2 tablespoons
  • Parsley and salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Rinse the barley under running water.
  2. In a pot, let the onion and speck soften in the oil.
  3. Add the diced vegetables and let them infuse 5 minutes.
  4. Add the barley and the hot broth and cook over low heat for about 1 hour, stirring occasionally.
  5. Add the parsley, adjust the salt and let rest 10 minutes before serving.
Chef's tip. It should be thick: if it dries out too much, add a ladle of broth.
