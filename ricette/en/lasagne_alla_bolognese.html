Journey city: Bologna

Lasagne alla bolognese

Servings6
Prep1 h 30 min
Cook51 min
Rest40 min
DifficultyHard

Ingredients

  • Type 00 flour · 300 g
  • Eggs · 2
  • Boiled spinach, well squeezed · 150 g
  • Bolognese ragù (see Tagliatelle al ragù) · 800 g
  • Whole milk (béchamel) · 1 liter
  • Butter (béchamel) · 80 g
  • Flour (béchamel) · 80 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano · 150 g
  • Nutmeg and salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Green pasta sheet: chop the spinach very fine and knead with flour and eggs. Rest 30 minutes, roll out thin and cut rectangles.
  2. Blanch the rectangles 1 minute in salted water and cool them in cold water.
  3. Béchamel: melt the butter, add the flour and stir 2 minutes. Pour in the hot milk a little at a time, stirring, and cook until thickened. Add salt and nutmeg.
  4. In a buttered dish, layer béchamel, pasta, ragù, béchamel and Parmigiano. Repeat for 5–6 layers.
  5. Finish with ragù, béchamel, Parmigiano and a few flakes of butter.
  6. Bake at 180 °C for 30–40 minutes, until a golden crust forms.
  7. Rest 10 minutes before cutting.
Chef's tip. The traditional Bolognese lasagna is green, with spinach in the pasta.
Did you know? The green spinach pasta is the Bolognese signature: elsewhere in Italy, lasagna is made with plain yellow pasta.

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk, Celery, Sulphites
