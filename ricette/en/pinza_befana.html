Befana's pinza

In Veneto and Friuli, pinza is baked for Epiphany, when big «panevin» bonfires are lit in the fields to say goodbye to the holidays and see which way the smoke blows.

Servings10
Prep30 min
Cook1 h 10 min
Rest30 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Gluten, Milk

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Cornmeal · 250 g
  • Flour · 250 g
  • Milk · 1 litre
  • Sugar · 150 g
  • Butter · 80 g
  • Raisins · 150 g
  • Dried figs · 150 g
  • Grappa · 3 tbsp
  • Fennel seeds · 1 tsp
  • Orange zest · zest of 1
  • Baking powder · 16 g

Step by step

  1. Soak the raisins and chopped figs in the grappa for 30 minutes.
  2. Bring the milk to the boil with the sugar and butter, pour in the cornmeal while stirring and cook for 10 minutes, like a soft polenta.
  3. Off the heat, add the flour with the baking powder, the fruit with its grappa, the fennel seeds and the orange zest, and mix well.
  4. Pour into a buttered tin and bake at 180 °C for about an hour, until the top is dark and crisp.
Chef's tip. Pinza is even better the next day, cut into squares and served cold.
