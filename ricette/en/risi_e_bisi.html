Risi e bisi

It was the dish offered to the Doge on April 25, the feast of St. Mark, patron of Venezia.

Servings4
Prep30 min
Cook1 h 28 min
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Milk

Ingredients

  • Fresh peas in the pod · 1.5 kg (500 g shelled)
  • Vialone Nano rice · 300 g
  • Onion · 1 small
  • Pancetta · 50 g
  • Butter · 50 g
  • Grated Parmesan · 50 g
  • Chopped parsley · 1 tablespoon
  • Salt and pepper · to taste

Step by step

  1. Shell the peas. Wash the pods and cook them in 1.5 liters of salted water for 1 hour, then blend and strain: that is the broth.
  2. In a pot, let the chopped onion and pancetta soften with half the butter.
  3. Add the peas and cook 5 minutes with a little broth.
  4. Add the rice and the hot broth a little at a time, cooking about 18 minutes.
  5. Turn off and stir in the remaining butter, the Parmigiano and the parsley. It must stay softer than a risotto.
Chef's tip. Use fresh spring peas: the pods give all the flavor.
