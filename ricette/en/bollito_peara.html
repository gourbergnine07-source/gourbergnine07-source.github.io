Boiled meats with pearà

«Pearà» means «peppered» in the Verona dialect: pepper is what makes it unique, and in Verona there's no festive bollito without it.

Servings6
Prep20 min
Cook3 h
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Gluten, Milk, Celery

Ingredients

  • Beef (brisket or shank) · 1 kg
  • Hen or capon · 1/2
  • Onion · 1
  • Carrots · 2
  • Celery · 1 stalk
  • Breadcrumbs · 200 g
  • Beef bone marrow · 80 g
  • Butter · 30 g
  • Meat broth · 1 litre
  • Black pepper · plenty
  • Grated Parmigiano · 40 g

Step by step

  1. Boil the beef and hen as for a bollito: water with the vegetables, meat added when it boils, and about 3 hours over low heat.
  2. For the pearà, melt the marrow with the butter in an earthenware pot, add the breadcrumbs and stir until absorbed.
  3. Pour in the hot broth a little at a time and cook on very low heat for about 2 hours, stirring often, until it becomes a thick cream.
  4. At the end add plenty of ground black pepper and the Parmigiano, and serve the pearà hot with the sliced meats.
Chef's tip. If you can't find marrow, you can use butter only: the pearà will be a little less rich, but still delicious.
