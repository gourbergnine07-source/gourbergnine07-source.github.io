Ossobuco with gremolada

In Milanese dialect «ossobuco» means «bone with a hole»: the hole full of marrow.

Servings4
Prep15 min
Cook1 h 40 min
DifficultyMedium

Allergens: Gluten, Milk, Sulphites, Celery

Ingredients

  • Veal shanks (ossobuco cuts) · 4
  • Flour · 30 g
  • Butter · 50 g
  • Onion · 1
  • Dry white wine · 150 ml
  • Meat stock · 500 ml
  • Grated zest of an untreated lemon · 1
  • Chopped parsley · 1 bunch
  • Garlic · 1 clove
  • Salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Nick the membrane around the shanks so they don't curl, then dust them lightly with flour.
  2. Brown the shanks in the butter with the chopped onion for 10 minutes, 5 per side.
  3. Pour in the wine, add a ladle of stock, cover and cook over low heat for 1 and a half hours, turning them halfway and adding stock if needed.
  4. Chop the lemon zest, parsley and garlic together: this is the gremolada.
  5. Turn off the heat, sprinkle the gremolada over the shanks, cover for a moment and serve, perhaps with risotto alla milanese.
Chef's tip. The marrow inside the bone is the tastiest part: enjoy it with a teaspoon.
