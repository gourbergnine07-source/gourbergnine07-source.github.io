Passatelli in brodo

They were born to use stale bread and leftover cheese: few ingredients, much flavor.

Servings4
Prep20 min
Cook2 min
Rest30 min
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk, Celery

Save on Pinterest

Ingredients

  • Breadcrumbs · 150 g
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano · 150 g
  • Eggs · 3
  • Nutmeg · 1 pinch
  • Lemon zest (optional) · to taste
  • Meat broth · 2 liters

Step by step

  1. Knead breadcrumbs, Parmigiano, eggs, nutmeg and lemon zest into a firm dough.
  2. Wrap it in film and rest 30 minutes in the fridge.
  3. Bring the broth to a boil.
  4. Press the dough with the special iron or a coarse-hole potato masher, directly over the broth, cutting cylinders of about 4 cm.
  5. Cook 1–2 minutes, until they float, and serve immediately.
Chef's tip. They are typical of all Emilia-Romagna, especially in winter.
