Patate in tecia

Servings4
Prep10 min
Cook40 min
DifficultyEasy

Ingredients

  • Potatoes · 1 kg
  • Onion · 1
  • Pancetta (optional) · 80 g
  • Broth · 200 ml
  • Lard or oil · 3 tablespoons
  • Cumin (optional) · 1 pinch
  • Salt and pepper · to taste

Step by step

  1. Boil the potatoes in their skins for about 20 minutes, until cooked but still firm.
  2. Peel and cut them into pieces.
  3. In a pan, let the onion (and the pancetta) soften in the lard.
  4. Add the potatoes and crush them roughly with a spoon, turning often and adding a little broth.
  5. Cook 15–20 minutes, until a golden crust forms. Salt and pepper.
Chef's tip. The secret is the crust: don't stir too often in the last minutes.
Did you know? 'Tecia' in the Trieste dialect means 'pan'.

Allergens: Celery
