Tagliatelle al ragù

Servings4
Prep1 h
Cook2 h 26 min
Rest30 min
DifficultyMedium

Ingredients

  • Type 00 flour (for the tagliatelle) · 400 g
  • Eggs (for the tagliatelle) · 4
  • Coarsely ground beef (chuck or belly) · 300 g
  • Streaky pancetta · 150 g
  • Carrot · 50 g
  • Celery · 50 g
  • Onion · 50 g
  • Tomato purée or peeled tomatoes · 300 g
  • Dry white wine · 1/2 glass
  • Whole milk · 1/2 glass
  • Meat broth · to taste
  • Grated Parmigiano Reggiano · to taste
  • Salt and pepper · to taste

Step by step

  1. Ragù: in a pan, melt the chopped pancetta. Add the finely chopped carrot, celery and onion and let soften for 10 minutes.
  2. Add the meat and brown it, stirring, until it sizzles and loses its pink color.
  3. Deglaze with the wine and let evaporate.
  4. Add the tomato, cover and cook over the lowest heat for about 2 hours, adding a little broth if it dries out too much.
  5. Towards the end add the milk, salt and pepper.
  6. Tagliatelle: make a well with the flour, break the eggs in the middle and knead 10 minutes until smooth.
  7. Wrap and rest 30 minutes.
  8. Roll out the pasta thin (about 1 mm), let it dry a few minutes, roll it up and cut into strips about 7 mm wide.
  9. Cook the tagliatelle 2–3 minutes in salted boiling water, drain and dress with the ragù and Parmigiano.
Chef's tip. True Bolognese ragù is never served with spaghetti: it goes with fresh tagliatelle.
Did you know? In 1982, the Italian Academy of Cuisine deposited the ragù bolognese recipe at the Chamber of Commerce of Bologna.

Allergens: Gluten, Eggs, Milk, Celery, Sulphites
