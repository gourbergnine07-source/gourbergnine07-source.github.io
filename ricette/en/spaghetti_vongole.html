Spaghetti with clams

In Naples, Christmas Eve dinner is meatless: spaghetti with clams is one of the best-loved first courses of the evening.

Servings4
Prep15 min
Cook15 min
Rest2 h
DifficultyEasy

Allergens: Gluten, Molluscs

Ingredients

  • Spaghetti · 320 g
  • Carpet-shell clams · 1 kg
  • Garlic · 2 cloves
  • Extra virgin olive oil · 4 tbsp
  • Chilli · to taste
  • Parsley · 1 handful
  • Salt · to taste

Step by step

  1. Soak the clams in cold salted water for at least 2 hours so they release their sand, then rinse them.
  2. In a wide pan, warm the oil with the garlic and chilli, add the clams and cover: they open in 3–4 minutes. Discard any that stay closed.
  3. Cook the spaghetti in lightly salted water and drain them a couple of minutes early.
  4. Finish cooking the spaghetti in the pan with the clams and their juices, then add chopped parsley and a drizzle of oil.
Chef's tip. Don't salt the pasta water too much: the clams already taste of the sea.
